💥 Sphere vs Cube. Cross vs Tetrahedron. Which shape wins?

🎯 HOW TO PLAY
Pick your shape, plan your shot with the aim line and fling it into the arena. Then physics takes over: your shape bounces off walls, unleashes attacks and slams into opponents. The harder the hit, the more damage! With a little luck, it dodges in slow motion.

• Ranked battles and stadium tournaments
